Free hosted widgets provide complete results on the embedding website, the NutriFit logo, sources and voluntary links. Catalog calculators run the existing local formulas and questionnaires; their public PDF is a snapshot of displayed values. The dish calculator uses the server food catalog, calculation and fresh PDF; CSV is local. MIT covers code, not trademarks, service quotas or rights to clinical instruments. No account is required for free results.

Paid hosted white label uses a verified exact HTTPS domain and integrationId with server-selected customer branding. Catalog white label omits the NutriFit PDF button; dish PDF/CSV uses customer branding. Native React is specifically the dish calculator and requires a native-enabled plan and server key. A personal Premium plan does not include widgets.

Activate configured plans in the account. Stripe grants access only after verified payment events; renewal cancellation keeps the paid period. Wallet purchases use available USD balance for one calendar month; supported crypto top-ups fund that balance. Bank transfers require support to record a confirmed payment. Plan prices, site limits and monthly operations are configured by NutriFit. No default trial, grace, overage, SLA or automatic wallet renewal is promised. DNS TXT verifies domains. Key rotation and disablement invalidate prior sessions; quotas reset each UTC calendar month. Formula interactions do not consume the nutrition operation quota.

An iframe protects its document from embedding-site CSS, but cannot prevent cropping or covering. Attribution and referrals are measurable benefits, not guaranteed SEO or revenue. Events contain lifecycle information only, no visitor answers or nutrient values.
